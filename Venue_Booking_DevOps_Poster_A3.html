1
The Problem
Manual campus venue scheduling relies on spreadsheets and paper forms, causing double-bookings, booking collisions, and lack of real-time slot visibility across facilities.
  • No real-time visibility into slot availability across auditoriums, seminar halls, and computer labs.
  • Paper tokens and manual approvals cause scheduling conflicts and duplicate venue allocations.
  • Risky manual deployments on production servers with prolonged downtime and no automated rollback.

On Failure: Build turns red immediately; packaging and container deployment are completely halted. JUnit reports and Selenium failure screenshots are automatically archived. Broken builds never reach production users.
Ansible IaC: playbook.yml configures packages, users, firewall, and deploys the container. Re-running yields changed=0 (idempotent). rollback.yml restores the previous stable release container in 1 automated step.

Key DevOps Concepts Shown
  • Continuous Integration: Every push is compiled and verified through 115 tests.
  • Continuous Delivery: Verified artifacts automatically packaged as Docker images.
  • Pipeline as Code: Versioned Jenkinsfile controls entire delivery lifecycle.
  • Quality Gate: Broken tests stop deployment; defect-free releases guaranteed.
  • IaC & Idempotency: Repeatable Ansible runs converge safely with changed=0.

Learnings & Future Scope
  • Fast Feedback: Regressions are caught in 28 seconds before reaching users.
  • Automated Recovery: Rollback script restores previous stable release in 1 command.
  • Production Hardening: Secure systemd isolation and health check probes.
  • Future Work: Kubernetes orchestration (K8s/Helm), Prometheus & Grafana monitoring, and OAuth2/SSO campus identity integration.
