1 THE PROBLEM

Students and faculty organizing academic seminars have no shared live view of venue occupancy, and manual releases risk breaking live operations.

  • No visibility of room occupancy or hourly slots, causing constant phone inquiries.
  • Paper & spreadsheet records cause double-booking collisions, lost reservations, and unclear status ownership.
  • Risky manual deploys via SSH that are slow, error-prone, and lack automated rollback.

9 KEY DEVOPS CONCEPTS SHOWN
  • CI: Every git push is compiled and tested automatically.
  • CD: Passing builds ship as a live container on Ubuntu VM.
  • Pipeline as Code: Jenkinsfile is versioned in GitHub.
  • Quality Gate: Failing tests abort packaging & deployment.
  • IaC + Idempotency: Playbook re-run reports changed=0.
  • Automated Rollback: Restores stable 1.0.0 release in 28s.
10 LEARNINGS & FUTURE SCOPE
  • Fast feedback: 115 tests catch regressions before users see them.
  • Rollback resilience: Bad releases are fixed with 1 command in 28s.
  • Storage persistence: DB survives container recreations.
